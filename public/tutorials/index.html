How a week works

Monday to FridayOne Machine Learning lesson and one Quantum Computing lesson, about 10 minutes each. Each week has one topic in each tutorial.
Every lessonAn idea with pictures, something to play with, real code with its real output, a small exercise, and three quick questions.
End of the weekAn optional five-minute flashback for each tutorial: flip cards and a mini quiz. Weekends never break your streak.
Run the codeEvery lesson comes with a Jupyter notebook: open it in Google Colab to run it free in your browser, or download it for your own Jupyter.

Sara Aminpour wrote these tutorials for a student starting research in quantum machine learning, and shares them here for anyone who wants to learn. Start anywhere: your progress is saved in this browser only.
