What do you remember?

What is an ansatz?tap to flip
A parameterized circuit template, for example layers of RYR_YRY​ rotations and CNOTs between neighbors: n(L+1)n(L + 1)n(L+1) knobs. Its expressibility is which states its knobs can reach; without entangling gates, only product states.
What shape is a cost along a single knob?tap to flip
A sine wave, acos⁡θ+bsin⁡θ+ca\cos\theta + b\sin\theta + cacosθ+bsinθ+c: three evaluations fix it. Landscapes can still have local minima, and shot noise blurs them.
The parameter-shift rule?tap to flip
∂θC=12 [ C(θ+π2)−C(θ−π2) ]\partial_\theta C = \frac12\,[\,C(\theta + \frac\pi2) - C(\theta - \frac\pi2)\,]∂θ​C=21​[C(θ+2π​)−C(θ−2π​)]: exact, robust to shot noise, two circuits per knob.
VQE in one line?tap to flip
Minimize the measured energy ⟨H⟩\langle H\rangle⟨H⟩ over an ansatz's knobs with a classical optimizer; by the variational principle every result is an upper bound on the ground-state energy.
Barren plateau?tap to flip
Slopes that shrink exponentially with the number of qubits for deep random circuits and global costs, until shot noise hides them. Local costs, shallow circuits and starts near the identity help.
The whole Quantum Computing tutorial, in one card?tap to flip
