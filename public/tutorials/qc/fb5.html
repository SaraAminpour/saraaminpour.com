Teleportation: what goes in, what comes out?tap to flip
In: one shared Bell pair and two classical bits sent from Alice to Bob. Out: Bob holds Alice's unknown qubit. Alice keeps only two random bits, so nothing was copied.
Alice reads m0m1=11m_0 m_1 = 11m0​m1​=11. What does Bob do?tap to flip
X (because m1=1m_1 = 1m1​=1), then Z (because m0=1m_0 = 1m0​=1). Before the bits arrive, his qubit is a perfectly random coin.
Superdense coding in one line?tap to flip
With a shared Bell pair, Alice applies I, X, Z or ZX to her half and sends that one qubit; Bob's CNOT and H read two bits.
What is phase kickback?tap to flip
With the output qubit in ∣−⟩|-\rangle∣−⟩, an oracle multiplies ∣x⟩|x\rangle∣x⟩ by (−1)f(x)(-1)^{f(x)}(−1)f(x): the answer becomes a sign on the input instead of a flipped output.
Deutsch–Jozsa vs Bernstein–Vazirani?tap to flip
Same circuit: H, one oracle query, H. DJ: 00…000\ldots000…0 means constant, anything else balanced. BV: the measured string is the secret sss of f(x)=s⋅x mod 2f(x) = s\cdot x \bmod 2f(x)=s⋅xmod2.
A Grover step?tap to flip
Oracle (flip the prize's sign), then diffuser (reflect every amplitude about the mean). Each step rotates the state by 2θ2\theta2θ, with sin⁡θ=1/N\sin\theta = 1/\sqrt{N}sinθ=1/N​; best after about π4N\frac{\pi}{4}\sqrt{N}4π​N​ steps.
