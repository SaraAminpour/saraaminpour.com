What do you remember?

What are the columns of a gate's matrix?tap to flip
Where it sends the basis states: the first column is U∣0⟩U|0\rangleU∣0⟩, the second is U∣1⟩U|1\rangleU∣1⟩.
A circuit applies A, then B, then C. Its matrix?tap to flip
CBACBACBA: written right to left, because A touches the state first.
What makes a matrix a valid gate?tap to flip
It's unitary: U†U=IU^\dagger U = IU†U=I. It keeps every state's length (total probability 1), and U†U^\daggerU† undoes it.
What is interference?tap to flip
Amplitudes for the same outcome add, and they can be negative, so routes can reinforce or cancel. H then H: the two routes to 1 cancel.
How do you measure in the X basis? In Y?tap to flip
X: apply H, then measure. Y: apply S†, then H, then measure. Each basis reads one coordinate: P=(1+coordinate)/2P = (1 + \text{coordinate})/2P=(1+coordinate)/2.
What is state tomography?tap to flip
Measuring many copies in the X, Y and Z bases to estimate all three coordinates of the Bloch arrow, which fixes the state.
