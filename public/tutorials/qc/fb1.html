The Born rule?tap to flip
Probability = amplitude's length squared: P(0)=∣α∣2P(0) = |\alpha|^2P(0)=∣α∣2, P(1)=∣β∣2P(1) = |\beta|^2P(1)=∣β∣2. In general, the chance of finding ∣ϕ⟩|\phi\rangle∣ϕ⟩ is ∣⟨ϕ∣ψ⟩∣2|\langle\phi|\psi\rangle|^2∣⟨ϕ∣ψ⟩∣2.
Global phase versus relative phase?tap to flip
A global phase (both arrows turned together) changes nothing measurable. A relative phase is real: ∣+⟩|+\rangle∣+⟩ and ∣−⟩|-\rangle∣−⟩ differ only in it.
What does a measurement do to a qubit?tap to flip
It gives one random bit, then collapses the qubit to the state you saw. Measuring again gives the same answer.
How does the error of an estimate shrink with shots NNN?tap to flip
Like 1/N1/\sqrt{N}1/N​: typical error ≈p(1−p)/N\approx\sqrt{p(1-p)/N}≈p(1−p)/N​. Four times the shots, half the error.
