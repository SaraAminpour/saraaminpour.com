What do you remember?

The four ways machines learn?tap to flip
Supervised: examples with answers. Unsupervised: no answers, find structure. Semi-supervised: a few answers plus lots of unlabeled data. Reinforcement: learn from rewards.
150 flowers with 4 measurements each: what is X.shape?tap to flip
(150, 4): one row per example, one column per feature. The labels live in a separate y of shape (150,).
kNN in three steps?tap to flip
Measure the distance to every training point, pick the kkk closest, vote. Small kkk: jagged boundary. Large kkk: smooth, maybe too smooth.
Why scale features before kNN?tap to flip
Distances add up squared differences. A feature with big numbers (a salary) drowns out one with small numbers (a height). Standardize: z=(x−μ)/σz = (x - \mu)/\sigmaz=(x−μ)/σ.
Training, validation and test sets: what is each for?tap to flip
Training: the model learns from it. Validation (or cross-validation): choose the knobs, like kkk. Test: report the final score, once.
Precision versus recall?tap to flip
Precision = TP / (TP + FP): when it says positive, how often it's right. Recall = TP / (TP + FN): of the real positives, how many it found.
