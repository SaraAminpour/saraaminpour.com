What does the MSE measure?tap to flip
The mean squared error: the average of (yi−y^i)2(y_i - \hat y_i)^2(yi​−y^​i​)2, the squared gaps between data and predictions. Outliers count a lot, because their gaps get squared.
What is the loss bowl?tap to flip
The MSE drawn over every pair of knobs (w,b)(w, b)(w,b). Each point is one line; the bottom of the bowl is the least-squares line.
The gradient-descent update rule?tap to flip
w←w−η dLdww \leftarrow w - \eta\,\frac{dL}{dw}w←w−ηdwdL​: step against the slope, scaled by the learning rate η\etaη. Too big diverges, too small crawls.
The five lines of a PyTorch training loop?tap to flip
zero_grad() → model(X) → loss_fn(...) → loss.backward() → optimizer.step().
Bias versus variance?tap to flip
Bias: the average fit misses the truth (too simple). Variance: fits change a lot from one training set to another (too flexible). Error = bias² + variance + noise.
Ridge versus lasso?tap to flip
Both penalize big weights. Ridge (λ∑w2\lambda\sum w^2λ∑w2) shrinks them all; lasso (λ∑∣w∣\lambda\sum |w|λ∑∣w∣) sets many exactly to zero. Choose λ\lambdaλ by cross-validation.
