Three fixes when one class is rare?tap to flip
Class weights in the loss, resampling the training set, or moving the threshold. All trade precision for recall.
How does a tree choose its question?tap to flip
It tries every feature and threshold and keeps the split with the lowest weighted Gini impurity 1−p2−(1−p)21 - p^2 - (1-p)^21−p2−(1−p)2.
What makes a random forest random?tap to flip
Each tree trains on a bootstrap resample and picks each split among random features. Their averaged vote cuts variance.
