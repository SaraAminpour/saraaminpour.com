Why add-one smoothing?tap to flip
Without it, a word never seen in one class has probability 0 there, and that single word overrules all the others. Pretend every word was seen once more.
Boosting versus a random forest?tap to flip
Forest: deep trees side by side on resamples, averaged (less variance). Boosting: shallow trees one after another, each fitting the team's residuals (less bias); stop in time.
Single, complete, average or Ward linkage?tap to flip
Single: nearest pair, follows chains. Complete: farthest pair, compact. Average: in between. Ward: least added spread, round even groups. Cut the dendrogram to get clusters.
DBSCAN's three kinds of points?tap to flip
Core: at least min_samples points within eps. Border: within eps of a core point. Noise: everything else, labeled −1.
What does a t-SNE map keep, and what not?tap to flip
It keeps who is near whom. Island sizes, gaps between islands and the axes mean little; another random start rearranges the map.
