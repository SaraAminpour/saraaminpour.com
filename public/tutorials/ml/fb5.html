What do you remember?

What does one neuron compute?tap to flip
A weighted sum plus a bias, passed through an activation: a=f(w⋅x+b)a = f(w \cdot x + b)a=f(w⋅x+b). With a sigmoid, that's logistic regression; with a step, it's the perceptron.
Why can't one neuron learn XOR?tap to flip
It draws a single straight line, and no straight line separates XOR's two classes. The perceptron rule never settles.
Why do networks need an activation function?tap to flip
Without the bend, a stack of linear layers is just one linear map, so the boundary stays straight. tanh⁡\tanhtanh and ReLU let it curve.
Backpropagation in one line?tap to flip
Run forward and store every value; then walk back from the loss, multiplying by each step's local slope (the chain rule). One backward pass gives every gradient.
Mini-batch, epoch, Adam, early stopping?tap to flip
Step with the gradient of a small random batch; an epoch is one pass through the data; Adam gives each weight its own step size; stop when the validation loss stops improving and keep the best weights.
What is distribution shift?tap to flip
Test data that comes from a different source than the training data, like finger-drawn digits for a network trained on scanned pen digits. Accuracy drops.
