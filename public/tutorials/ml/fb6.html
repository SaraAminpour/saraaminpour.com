What do you remember?

k-means in one line, and its two weak spots?tap to flip
Assign each point to its nearest center, move each center to its points' mean, repeat. Weak spots: bad starts (use k-means++ and restarts) and non-round groups.
Elbow and silhouette?tap to flip
Inertia always falls with kkk: pick the elbow where it stops falling fast. The silhouette b−amax⁡(a,b)\frac{b - a}{\max(a, b)}max(a,b)b−a​ compares own-cluster and nearest-other-cluster distances: higher is better.
E-step and M-step?tap to flip
E: each point's probability for each blob (Bayes' rule: weight × bell curve, normalized). M: refit each blob's weight, mean and covariance from the weighted points.
What does PCA keep, and what does it lose?tap to flip
It keeps the directions of largest variance (eigenvectors of the covariance). Variance kept plus average squared rebuild error always equals the total variance.
Three uses of an autoencoder?tap to flip
Compression (the bottleneck code), generation (decode any point of the code space), and anomaly detection (large reconstruction error). Noisy in, clean out makes a denoiser.
When does semi-supervised learning work?tap to flip
When classes are dense regions separated by gaps (smoothness and cluster assumptions). Label spreading follows that shape; self-training risks confirmation bias.
