What is a feature map?tap to flip

What are support vectors?tap to flip
The points on the edges of the SVM's street. They alone fix the boundary; moving any other point changes nothing.
What does CCC do in a soft-margin SVM?tap to flip
Sets the price of points inside the street. Small CCC: wide, forgiving street. Large CCC: narrow street, tries to get every training point right.

A kernel matrix that is nearly the identity means…tap to flip
Every point only looks like itself: too narrow a kernel (huge γ\gammaγ). The SVM can only memorize.
