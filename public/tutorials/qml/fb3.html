The three parts of a variational quantum classifier?tap to flip
Encode the point as angles, process it with trainable rotations and entangling gates, and read out ⟨Z⟩\langle Z\rangle⟨Z⟩. Predict class 1 when ⟨Z⟩<0\langle Z\rangle < 0⟨Z⟩<0.
Circuit runs per epoch?tap to flip
N(1+2P)N(1 + 2P)N(1+2P) for NNN points and PPP weights: one run for each prediction, two shifted runs per weight for the gradient. On hardware, every run is hundreds of shots.
What did the CNOT do on XOR?tap to flip
Without it, the measured qubit only ever sees its own feature, so the boundary depends on x2x_2x2​ alone. Entangling gates let features interact.
From counts to a probability?tap to flip
p(class 1)=1−⟨Z⟩2≈n1Sp(\text{class }1) = \frac{1 - \langle Z\rangle}{2} \approx \frac{n_1}{S}p(class 1)=21−⟨Z⟩​≈Sn1​​. Train it with cross-entropy, sharpen it with a classical scale σ(−a⟨Z⟩+b)\sigma(-a\langle Z\rangle + b)σ(−a⟨Z⟩+b), and read four classes from the four bitstrings of two qubits.
How much do shots blur a prediction?tap to flip
The estimate of ⟨Z⟩\langle Z\rangle⟨Z⟩ wobbles by (1−⟨Z⟩2)/S\sqrt{(1 - \langle Z\rangle^2)/S}(1−⟨Z⟩2)/S​: about 0.32 with 10 shots, 0.1 with 100. Only points near the boundary flip, and halving the wobble costs four times the shots.
How does a circuit become a PyTorch layer?tap to flip
Forward runs the circuits; backward uses the parameter-shift rule for the weights and for the input angles, so the gradient can flow on into classical layers.
Who did the work in the digits hybrid?tap to flip
The classical encoder. A classical twin matched the hybrid at 99%, and with the encoder frozen, the quantum and classical versions both got stuck at 78%.
Why do uploads matter more than layers?tap to flip
A circuit with LLL uploads of xxx is a Fourier series with frequencies up to LLL. Trainable gates only set the amounts; only more uploads add frequencies.
Quantum classifier versus a tiny network?tap to flip
A tie within the spread on moons, circles and XOR, but 120,000 circuit runs (120 million shots) against about 650,000 multiply-adds.
