What do you remember?

Shrink, shift or fade?tap to flip
Gate noise shrinks ⟨Z⟩\langle Z\rangle⟨Z⟩ toward 0: signs survive, but shots suffer. Relaxation and readout errors shift it: re-tune on the device. In deep noisy circuits, gradients fade exponentially.
How do you undo a shift?tap to flip
Learn the threshold, or the shift bbb in σ(−a⟨Z⟩+b)\sigma(-a\langle Z\rangle + b)σ(−a⟨Z⟩+b), from the noisy device's outputs on training data. It brought the damped classifier back from 0.80 to full accuracy.
Trainable, therefore imitable?tap to flip
The known cures for barren plateaus keep the computation in a small part of the state space, and that part can often be simulated or imitated classically.
A Fourier surrogate?tap to flip
A circuit with LLL uploads per feature is a linear model on cos⁡(ω⃗⋅x⃗)\cos(\vec\omega \cdot \vec x)cos(ω⋅x) and sin⁡(ω⃗⋅x⃗)\sin(\vec\omega \cdot \vec x)sin(ω⋅x) for frequencies up to LLL: 49 features for 2 inputs and L=3L = 3L=3. Logistic regression on them matched or beat the circuit.
The Helstrom bound?tap to flip
From one copy, no measurement tells ρ0\rho_0ρ0​ from ρ1\rho_1ρ1​ (equal odds) with probability above 12(1+D)\frac{1}{2}(1 + D)21​(1+D), where DDD is the trace distance. A trained measurement reached it exactly.
Why can measuring first be fatal?tap to flip
If the class lives in the phase, Z-basis records are fair coins for both classes: the best classical model on them is stuck at 50%, while the right measurement nearly always succeeds.
Questions for any QML claim?tap to flip
A strong, equally tuned baseline? Several splits, with spread? How much was tried before the reported result? Shots, noise and scale? Could a classical surrogate do it? Where did the data come from?
What did the ad hoc dataset show?tap to flip
A kernel matched to how the labels were made learns from few examples: 93% from 20 points, against 52% for a tuned RBF. A real effect, but a narrow one, not a general quantum advantage.
The parts of a mini-study?tap to flip
A question, a hypothesis written first, a fair protocol, numbers with their spread, an honest verdict, the limitations and a next step.
